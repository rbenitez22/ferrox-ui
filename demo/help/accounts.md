# Accounts

What an account is, and the kinds there are.

## Statement balance {#statement-balance}

The closing balance printed on the bank's statement for this period.

Reconciling ticks off entries until the account agrees with it.

Related: #split-amount, #account-kinds

## The five kinds of account {#account-kinds}

Every account is one of five kinds, and its kind decides which side makes it grow.

| Kind | Holds | Grows with |
|---|---|---|
| Asset | What you own | Debits |
| Liability | What you owe | Credits |
| Equity | What is left | Credits |
| Income | Money earned | Credits |
| Expense | Money spent | Debits |
